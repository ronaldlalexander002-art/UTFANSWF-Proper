# UTFANSWF Proper

## Unified Physics Operating Environment

UTFANSWF Proper, by Ronald L. Alexander, is a **Unified Physics Operating Environment (UPOE)**. It connects physics domains through shared states, constraints, translation rules, ownership boundaries and executable consistency checks.

It is not presented as a replacement for general relativity, quantum field theory, cosmology or effective field theory. Each domain retains its applicable physics; UTFANSWF evaluates how their connections behave together.

This repository is a public guide to the **already-published paper and reproducibility bundle**. It contains an overview and links only. The publication files remain on Zenodo; source code, datasets and executable materials are available through the published bundle linked below.

## Five connected layers

**Zero State (ZS) → SWF-ISM → ANSWF → SANSWF → UTFANSWF Proper**

1. **Zero State (ZS)** supplies the reference and initialization configuration.
2. **SWF-ISM** develops the information-state construction.
3. **ANSWF** establishes the low-energy effective-field-theory baseline and its admissibility conditions.
4. **SANSWF** develops symmetry, Lorentz and gauge consistency, bounded-from-below constraints and unitarity checks.
5. **UTFANSWF Proper** connects these structures across gravity, cosmology, particle physics, dark matter, baryon structure and computational applications.

Category–Functor Gate Logic (CFGL) carries system-level acceptance conditions across the connected architecture. An isolated passing calculation is not automatically sufficient for the coupled framework.

## What the published work covers

The paper and bundle connect the mathematical construction to executable calculations, including coupled matter and gravitational evolution, moving interfaces, parent–layer–child exchange, particle-response calculations and cosmological applications. Recorded inputs, conventions, results and acceptance conditions make the calculations available for examination and reproduction.

Prediction programs discussed in the published paper include:

- **NP-01:** the fixed-t0 light-quark nucleon response, evaluated within its stated conventions.
- **NP-02:** an exact-Z6 baryon-number selection rule. Under the adopted residual symmetry, ΔB = 1 and ΔB = 2 are forbidden, while ΔB = 3 is the first allowed baryon-violating class. This does not independently determine an absolute trinucleon lifetime.
- **NP-03:** a conditional PSN-v2 one-loop gauge-running forecast with split thresholds at approximately 1.816, 2.568 and 3.632 TeV.
- **Axion searches:** a candidate 6–60 μeV search interval, with a narrower 10–40 μeV conditional forecast. The published root comparison directly covers 10–60 μeV; the lower interval retains its stated coverage and input limitations.
- **Ringdown:** a canonical Kerr quasinormal-mode branch, without a required nonzero echo signal under its stated conditions.

The paper provides the assumptions and evidential boundaries for each result. These summaries are not additional predictions or replacements for the published calculations.
